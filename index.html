Bachay Mehfooz: a multilingual child-safety reporting app

A clickable concept for the paper on language access and child-friendly policing in Pakistan. Pick a language, report a concern with icons, and see where the report would be routed under existing law. Nothing is sent or stored.

Proposed routing under current law

Each icon in the app maps to an existing legal mechanism. The highlighted row follows the report type chosen in the prototype.

Report typeExisting mechanismLanguage gap the app targets

Design principle: the reporter never has to name a law or write in English or formal Urdu. The app does the classification, and staff see the report in the language of the reporter with a translation.

Prototype for research illustration only. The translations are drafts and must be checked by native speakers of each language before any user testing. The Balochi text is a first draft, and the Balti entry is a placeholder that reuses Urdu wording until a Balti speaker supplies the translation. Voice input is shown as a design element and does not record. If you are in danger now, call the police on 15.
